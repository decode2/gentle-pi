## Objective and problem
Require a host-enforced human decision before sending any cross-orchestrator message. Recipient selection is not consent; explicit recipient IDs and implicit selection must obey the same boundary. Approved issue: https://github.com/Gentleman-Programming/gentle-shell/issues/1364.
